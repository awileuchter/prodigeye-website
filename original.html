About ProdigEye

ProdigEye is a medical-grade, AI-powered Diagnostic platform designed to support clinicians in accurately identifying anterior segment eye diseases using only a standard smartphone camera. Built together by an AI expert and an ophthalmologist, ProdigEye aims to improve ophthalmic care and reduce delay in diagnosis in settings where specialist access and ocular equipment are limited.

Our Technology

ProdigEye delivers specialist-level eye diagnostics directly from a smartphone camera. Using AI-powered analysis, the platform provides accurate, early diagnosis at the point of care, reducing non-urgent emergency room referrals and lowering diagnostic uncertainty. By improving clinical confidence, ProdigEye unlocks significant efficiency and cost savings while enhancing patient health and outcomes.

Our Team

Dr. Moshe Fisher

Co-Founder & Chief Medical Officer

Dr. Fisher is an American board-certified Ophthalmologist specializing in Cornea & External Disease with 27 years of clinical experience. He earned his medical degree from Albert Einstein College of Medicine in New York, completed his Ophthalmology residency at Long Island Jewish Medical Center, and pursued an advanced fellowship in Cornea and External Disease at Mount Sinai–NYU. He previously served as Assistant Director of the Cornea Service and Assistant Clinical Professor at the University of Medicine & Dentistry of New Jersey. He is currently the Director of the Modiin Eye Center in Modiin, Israel.
