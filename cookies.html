How we use cookies and how you can manage your preferences

Last updated: February 2026  |  Effective date: February 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work correctly and to remember your preferences. This Cookie Policy explains how ProdigEye uses cookies, in compliance with the EU ePrivacy Directive and the GDPR.

Our approach: ProdigEye uses only the minimum cookies necessary for the website to function. We do not use advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies that identify or profile individual users.

3. What We Do Not Use

  • No Google Analytics or other analytics platforms
  • No advertising or retargeting cookies
  • No social media tracking pixels
  • No third-party behavioural profiling tools

4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete existing ones. Disabling the essential cookies above may affect basic website functionality.

For instructions on managing cookies in your browser, refer to your browser's help documentation.
