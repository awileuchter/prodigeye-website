Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal data

Last updated: February 2026  |  Effective date: February 2026

1. Introduction

ProdigEye Ltd. ("ProdigEye", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to transparency about how our website operates. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to data in full compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and all other applicable privacy laws.

Our core commitment: ProdigEye does not collect, store, or process any personal data from visitors to this website. We do not use forms that collect personal information, we do not require registration or login, and we do not track individual users.

2. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact details
  • Health or medical information of any kind
  • IP addresses stored in identifiable form
  • Device identifiers or persistent tracking identifiers
  • Location data
  • Behavioural or browsing profiles

The contact form on our website sends messages directly to our team and no submission data is stored on our servers or any third-party database.

3. Cookies

This website uses only essential technical cookies required for basic functionality. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party analytics cookies that identify individual users. Please refer to our Cookie Policy for full details.

4. Third-Party Services

This website uses Google Fonts to load typography. Google Fonts operates without cookies and does not track users. No other third-party services, scripts, or embeds are used that could collect personal data from visitors.

5. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. Since we do not collect any personal data, no data from minors is collected either.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, delete, or export. However, if you have any privacy-related questions or concerns, you are welcome to contact us and we will respond promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Since we do not collect contact details, we are unable to notify you directly of changes, so we encourage you to review this page periodically.
